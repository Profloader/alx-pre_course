What This Means for Your Book

When someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI Overviews a question in your area of expertise — leadership, health, business, theology, science — they receive an AI-generated answer. That answer cites sources. The sources cited are not necessarily the most famous. They are the most structured, signal-rich, and AI-readable.

Axitos is among the first book publishers to build Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) into its core publishing workflow — not as a post-launch add-on, but as a foundational layer woven into manuscript development, metadata, distribution, and content strategy from day one.

GEO — Generative Engine Optimization

We structure your manuscript, author pages, and book metadata so AI answer engines can parse, understand, and cite your work accurately. This includes schema markup, semantic structuring, and content formatted to directly answer questions your readers are already asking AI tools.

AEO — Answer Engine Optimization

We build your author authority profile across the web — so when ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI Overviews need a source in your topic area, your book and your name are part of the indexed landscape they draw from.

Common Questions

Honest Answers.
No Fine Print.

Is Axitos a traditional publisher — or self-publishing?

Axitos is a traditional publisher. We acquire manuscripts we believe in, fund their production and distribution, and earn through royalties. You pay nothing upfront. The critical distinction from vanity or hybrid publishing: we take on the financial risk because we believe in your book. If we don't acquire it, we don't publish it.

What does "no upfront fees" actually mean?

It means exactly that. Editing, cover design, layout, production, distribution setup, and our AI visibility marketing are all funded by Axitos. There is no submission fee, no publishing fee, and no hidden charge at any stage. Axitos earns when your book sells — and not before.

What is Print on Demand, and is it a disadvantage?

Print on Demand means books are printed as orders arrive rather than in large advance runs. For authors this is an advantage: your book is never out of stock, there is no overprint risk, and it remains available indefinitely. POD is the standard fulfillment infrastructure used by Ingram, which routes your book to Amazon, Barnes & Noble, and over 45,000 retailers globally. Quality is comparable to offset printing for most trade book formats.

What is AI visibility marketing, and does it actually work?

AI visibility marketing is the practice of structuring your book, author profile, and metadata so that AI answer engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews — can find, understand, and reference your work. Industry data shows visitors referred from ChatGPT convert at approximately 15–16%, compared to 1–2% for Google organic search — roughly a 9× advantage (Seer Interactive, June 2025). AI-referred traffic to retail websites grew 1,200% year-over-year by early 2025 (Adobe Analytics, March 2025). Axitos positions your book to benefit from this shift from day one.

Do I keep my copyright?

Yes. Your intellectual property is yours. You grant Axitos a license to publish, distribute, and market your book. You retain full copyright ownership throughout. License terms are established in your publishing agreement and can be negotiated before signing.

What royalty rate can I expect?

Authors on the Traditional Publishing Partnership earn 30% royalties on net receipts from book sales. Authors on the Premium Publishing Partnership earn 50% royalties on net receipts. Net receipts means after the retailer's share — standard practice across the publishing industry. Traditional Partnership authors also receive author copies at a 50% discount to support their promotional efforts.

Can I submit an AI-written manuscript?

No. Axitos does not publish AI-generated manuscripts. Authors may use AI tools as a drafting or editing aid, but the creative work must be substantively and originally human. All submissions pass an AI-content review as part of editorial evaluation. This protects the integrity of your book and Axitos's standing as a credible source within AI citation systems.

How does the Premium Publishing Partnership differ from the Traditional Partnership?

In the Traditional Publishing Partnership, authors actively promote their book through their existing platform and network — that contribution is a core part of the agreement. Authors on this track earn 30% net royalties, and receive author copies at a 50% discount to fuel their promotional efforts.

In the Premium Publishing Partnership, Axitos takes on the majority of the publishing and marketing workload — particularly around AI visibility and content marketing — while authors still put their name behind their work, review and approve edits, and provide the materials and access we need to represent them well. Think of it as Axitos doing the heavy lifting in the engine room while you remain the public face and authority of your book. Premium Partners earn 50% net royalties. This model involves a single upfront investment and is offered to a very limited number of authors per year. Indicate your interest in the query form below and our team will follow up directly.
